## 7. Key Evidence-Based Recommendations

Based on the analysis above:

1. **Frustrated sellers** need slower pace, warmer tone, simpler language
2. **Large businesses** respond better to formal, value-focused communication
3. **Small businesses** respond to warm, relatable Hinglish at medium pace
4. **Negative/resistant sellers** need patient, empathetic approach with specific value props
5. **Hindi-dominant calls** (Hinglish mix) have higher engagement overall
6. **Short calls** (<30s) indicate rushed sellers who need direct, efficient communication
