## Key Design Principles Demonstrated
1. **Geographic Distinctness:** Southern sellers receive Indian English / neutral diction; traditional Hindi-belt traders receive pure Hindi / localized Hinglish.
2. **Turnover-Tiered Formality:** Large turnover (5-25 Cr) receives formal corporate framing (`formal_corporate`); small proprietors receive respectful peer warmth (`respectful_warm`).
3. **Behavioral De-escalation:** Sellers with past frustration receive slower, empathetic calming registers rather than aggressive sales pitches.
