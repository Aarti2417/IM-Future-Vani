## Verification Standard
All buyer inquiries on IndiaMART are verified using:
1. **Mobile OTP Verification (100%):** Buyer must authenticate with a one-time password before an inquiry is dispatched to a seller.
2. **Business Credibility Check:** GST, company name, and inquiry specification verification to minimize spam.
