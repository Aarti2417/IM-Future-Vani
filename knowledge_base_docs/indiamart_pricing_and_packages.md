# IndiaMART Official Membership Packages & Pricing Rate Card

## Overview
IndiaMART offers three primary membership tiers tailored to MSME turnover and sales requirements. All prices are transparent and baseline rates.

---

## 1. Mini Leader Package
- **Target Audience:** Micro & Small MSMEs (Annual turnover under ₹1.5 Cr).
- **Annual Cost:** ₹28,000 + 18% GST (Total ₹33,040).
- **Monthly Equivalent:** Approx ₹2,000 to ₹2,500 per month (₹2,333/month baseline).
- **Monthly Verified Lead Quota:** 45 high-intent, OTP-verified buyer buy-leads per month.
- **Key Features:**
  - Priority search listing in top local districts.
  - Direct telephone calls from OTP-verified buyers.
  - IndiaMART verified seller trust logo.
  - Free digital catalog setup and high-resolution photo uploads.
  - Direct SMS & WhatsApp lead notifications.

---

## 2. TrustSEAL Verified Package
- **Target Audience:** Medium MSMEs (Annual turnover ₹1.5 Cr – ₹5 Cr).
- **Annual Cost:** ₹48,000 + 18% GST (Total ₹56,640).
- **Monthly Equivalent:** Approx ₹4,000 per month.
- **Monthly Verified Lead Quota:** 90 OTP-verified buyer buy-leads per month.
- **Key Features:**
  - TrustSEAL background verification badge (boosts buyer trust by 4x).
  - State-level top priority search ranking.
  - Instant lead alerts via WhatsApp and mobile app.
  - Payment escrow protection via Pay with IndiaMART.

---

## 3. Maximiser Star Package
- **Target Audience:** Large & Enterprise Manufacturers (Annual turnover ₹5 Cr+).
- **Annual Cost:** ₹85,000 + 18% GST (Total ₹1,00,300).
- **Monthly Equivalent:** Approx ₹7,083 per month.
- **Monthly Verified Lead Quota:** 180+ verified buyer buy-leads per month.
- **Key Features:**
  - Pan-India top 3 guaranteed placement across all target keywords.
  - Dedicated Relationship Manager (RM) assigned to the account.
  - Unlimited verified buy-lead consumption.
  - Export lead access and multi-city national targeting.
